This project studies fantasy baseball snake-draft roster construction as an operations research problem. We formulate an ADP-aware integer linear program as an exact benchmark that maximizes projected roster points subject to roster requirements, player eligibility, snake-draft picks, and ADP availability. Because the exact model becomes large as the player pool and roster size grow, we design Opportunity Cost Greedy, a scalable heuristic that compares the best currently available player at each open position with the best player expected to remain at the next owned pick. Direct Greedy is used as a simple benchmark heuristic. Synthetic N1-N6 experiments test point distributions, position distributions, ADP uncertainty, and large-scale instance growth, while 2026 Yahoo and FanGraphs data provide realistic validation. Opportunity Cost Greedy consistently has smaller optimality gaps than Direct Greedy. In the largest synthetic stress instance, both heuristics finish within seconds, while the ADP-aware ILP is manually interrupted without output.
